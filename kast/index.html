Unit Economics

The Headline Number

$100M ARR ÷ 1M users = $100 ARPU. Nubank at scale earns $30–50/user. Revolut ~$60. $100 ARPU would be exceptional — implying a self-selecting base of high-activity users who've funded, activated, and spend regularly. True active users may be 200–300K (20–30% MAU rate typical for neobanks), making active ARPU closer to $400.

Revenue Streams by Priority

  • 1. Membership subscriptions (highest margin): Premium $1K/yr, Private $10K/yr. Near-zero variable cost. This line is what makes cashback viable. Without subscription revenue, cashback creates negative unit economics at scale.
  • 2. Card interchange: ~0.5–0.8% per transaction. At $5B annualized volume, ~$25M gross. Net interchange after cashback payout is thin or negative at premium tiers — subscriptions must cover the gap.
  • 3. FX conversion fees: 0.5–1.75% on non-USD spend. Zero for USD purchases. Meaningful for LatAm users spending in local currency.
  • 4. Deposit + transaction fees: ACH $2, FedWire $15, non-stablecoin crypto conversion 2–5%, ATM $3 + 2%, declined transaction $0.50, inactivity $1/month.
  • 5. Payout fees: Global bank payouts. Corridor-variable.
  • 6. Earn (future): Currently 0% fee, 100% yield to users. Optionality: 0.5% fee on $1B TVL = $5M/year at near-zero marginal cost.
  • 7. Float / treasury (implicit): T-bill yield on USDKY reserves, spread on stablecoin positions held in custody.

Risks

1 — Rain Dependency
Rain is KAST's Visa Principal Member. Rain's $1.95B valuation is 3.25x KAST's. Rain just became a Mastercard Principal Member too. Rain processes $3B+ for 200+ partners including Western Union. If Rain raises card program pricing, builds a competing consumer product, or realigns toward enterprise-only, KAST's card economics change. Mitigation: KAST's own USDK/USDKY stablecoin issuance, Privy wallet stack, own compliance build — but this takes 2–3 years.
2 — Fraud Trust Gap (Active Churn, Not Latent)
Bryan thread: 89K views, April 2026. Fraud → $30–50 chargeback fee → ghosted support → switch to Avici. Avici is marketing against KAST on this gap specifically. This is happening now, not as a future risk.
3 — Cashback Economics Require High Premium Penetration
Standard users generate ~$20–50/year net revenue (FX + ATM fees). $100M ARR target with 1M users needs either very high Premium/Private mix or massive Standard volume. Power user perception of 1.17% effective rate directly attacks the subscription upgrade funnel.
4 — LatAm Regulatory Patchwork
Brazil: stablecoins now classified as FX operations (Feb 2026). Potential $100K cap under consultation. Colombia: full DIAN crypto tax reporting. Argentina: capital controls. Mexico: stringent Fintech Law licensing. Country-by-country compliance at scale is expensive and operationally complex.
5 — LatAm-Native Competitors with Local Structural Advantages
Belo (Tether-led, $14M April 2026) explicitly expanding to Peru, Colombia, Mexico, Chile. Lemon has 130K+ active Earn users in Argentina. Nubank (90M users) now rewards USDC holding. The next wave of LatAm signups may go to locally-embedded products with lower regulatory friction.
6 — USDK/USDKY Stablecoin Issuance + GENIUS Act Compliance
Issuing own stablecoins post-GENIUS Act (enacted July 2025) means BSA obligations: AML, customer ID, sanctions verification. May require bank-level compliance infrastructure. Adds meaningful operational overhead before Earn monetization becomes possible.
7 — Earn Monetization Triggers Churn
Currently 0% fee, 100% yield to users. When fees are introduced, users earning 5% drop to 4.75% — noticeable. Timing and communication of this transition is a retention event, not a quiet product update.

Stablecoin Macro

2026 is the first cycle where stablecoins are not just an asset class — they're becoming a product primitive. The difference from previous cycles: regulation, institutional adoption, and consumer products are converging simultaneously.

Volume Reality

  • $33T in stablecoin on-chain volume 2025 (vs $25.5T Visa + Mastercard combined).
  • USDC + USDT: 95%+ market share.
  • $22T → $33T growth from 2024 to 2025.
  • Caveat: on-chain volume includes exchange transfers, DeFi, bot activity. Not all consumer payments. The correct read: stablecoins are becoming the settlement rail; KAST's job is to put consumer products on top.

GENIUS Act (July 2025)

  • US federal payment stablecoin framework.
  • Subjects issuers to Bank Secrecy Act: AML, sanctions, customer ID.
  • Net positive for serious players: regulatory clarity → institutional adoption → consumer trust.
  • Risk: compliance costs rise, Earn/yield products face stricter scrutiny.

Incumbent Moves

  • Visa: Stablecoin-linked cards via Bridge in LatAm — including Peru. $3.5B annualized USDC settlement.
  • Mastercard: Acquiring BVNK for up to $1.8B. Also now a Rain partner.
  • Western Union: USDPT stablecoin on Solana via Anchorage Digital, targeted May 2026 launch.
  • PayPal PYUSD: 4% yield to drive adoption. ~$4.1B market cap Apr 2026. Requires strong incentives — validates KAST's cashback approach, but also shows rewards competition is escalating.
  • Stripe: Stablecoin payment volume doubled to ~$400B, ~60% B2B flows.

Opportunity 01 · KYC
Single-pass onboarding with live status tracking
Signal: @Innerdevcrypto (40K followers), @Anon67890657, and the JD itself ("cut to under 2 minutes") all point to the same problem. Support re-requests documents already submitted. Status shows "completed" while review is ongoing. Bank detail flow is confusing.

Hypothesis: Pre-submission checklist that collects everything in one session + in-app status tracker with 4 clear states (Submitted → Under Review → Approved → Card Ready) + zero re-asks after first submission. Estimated impact: if KYC drop-off is 40% (typical for full-KYC crypto products), improving it by 30% = 12% more users reaching first-spend each month.
Opportunity 02 · Tier Upgrade
Personalized value calculator at the upgrade screen
Signal: @Innerdevcrypto ran the math live in a 40K-follower post. Effective Premium rate = 1.17%, Private = 0.92%. "Not a lot at all." Power users are ignoring KastPoints and Earn APY because they're not surfaced at the moment of decision.

Hypothesis: Replace "upgrade to Premium — 2% cashback" CTA with a personalized screen showing: annual USD cashback at your current spend level + KastPoints value in USD (at today's SOL price) + Earn APY on current balance + withdrawal limit increase + concierge access. Total annual value, personalized. A/B test this against current screen. Hypothesis: personalized total-value framing converts at 2× because it makes the full picture visible at decision moment.
Opportunity 03 · Fraud & Disputes
Self-serve dispute flow + auto-fraud protection
Signal: Bryan thread (89K views, April 2026) — $3,500 fraud, $30–50 chargeback fees, manual PDF process, explicit switch to Avici. Avici is winning churned KAST users specifically on this gap.

Hypothesis: OTP or location confirmation for first foreign transaction on a new device. Auto-freeze on velocity anomalies (5 transactions in 3 minutes in a different country). In-app dispute flow: tap transaction → dispute → photo evidence → submitted in under 2 minutes. Zero chargeback fee for first verified fraud incident per user. Expected outcome: reduce Bryan-class incidents by 60–80%, eliminate Avici as a named alternative in user complaints.
Opportunity 04 · Earn Vault
Withdrawal status transparency + real-time notifications

Hypothesis: Real-time withdrawal status with estimated time and chain confirmation link. Push notification at each stage: initiated → processing → complete. If stuck >30 minutes, proactive support message sent. Expected outcome: no user ever wonders if their money is lost. Reduces CEO escalations on vault issues by estimated 70%.
